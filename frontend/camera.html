Camera Basics
📸

How to Open Camera

Swipe up → tap Camera icon, or press double power button.

🎛️

Modes

Photo • Video • Portrait • Selfie • Panorama.

⚡

Flash

Use flash in low light: Auto • On • Off.

🔍

Zoom

Pinch in/out or tap 1x/2x for better focus.

✨

Clarity

Tap on the subject to focus & brighten.

🖼️

Where Photos Save?

Gallery → Camera folder.

Step-by-Step Guide
  1. Hold phone steady with both hands.
  2. Tap screen to focus.
  3. Use flash only when dark.
  4. For selfie → tap camera flip button.
  5. Press white circle to click photo.

Safety Tips
  • Do not click photos of documents (Aadhaar, PAN).
  • Do not share personal photos with strangers.
  • Do not open camera links sent by unknown numbers.
  • Keep gallery locked with PIN.
