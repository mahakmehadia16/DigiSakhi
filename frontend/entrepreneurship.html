Safety Tips (Avoid Scams)
  • Don’t pay “registration fee” for jobs.
  • Beware of buyers asking for OTP or remote app.
  • Use UPI QR; avoid sharing personal data.
  • Check courier tracking; keep receipts.
  • Use business number (or WA Business).
