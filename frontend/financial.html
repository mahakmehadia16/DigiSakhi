Digital Payments (UPI & QR)
📱

UPI

Instant money transfer using mobile apps.

  • Open Google Pay / PhonePe
  • Enter amount
  • Enter UPI PIN
🔳

QR Code

Scan to pay shopkeepers safely.

  • Open UPI app
  • Scan QR
  • Confirm amount
🆔

UPI ID

You can pay using UPI ID (example: name@bank).

👛

Wallets

Amazon Pay / Paytm for small payments.
