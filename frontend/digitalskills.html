📱 Lesson 1: Using Your Smartphone

Your smartphone is a powerful tool. Learn to use the home screen, download apps from the Play Store, manage storage, and set up a screen lock to keep your phone safe. Always keep your phone software updated to protect against viruses.

🌐 Lesson 2: Internet Safety Basics

Be careful about what you share online. Never share your Aadhaar number, OTP, bank details, or home address with strangers. Use strong passwords — mix letters, numbers and symbols. Enable two-factor authentication on important accounts like Google and email.

📧 Lesson 3: Email & Google Account

A Google account gives you access to Gmail, Google Maps, YouTube, and Google Pay. Create one at accounts.google.com. Use a strong password and add your phone number for recovery. Your email is your digital identity — keep it secure.

📷 Lesson 4: Camera & Photos

Your phone camera can scan QR codes for payments, document scanning, and video calls. Use Google Lens to identify objects, translate text, and search the internet using images. Back up your photos to Google Photos for free storage.
